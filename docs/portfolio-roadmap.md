trust. The repo proves competence but clients won't dig — proof must be
pulled out of the repo and onto the page.

## Owner decision gates

| Decision | Why it blocks |

| Commit to "replies within 24h" | A promise that must be kept |

## Explicit non-goals

- No invented metrics, testimonials, pilot results, or client names.
- No pricing tiers (scope varies per client).
- No emoji/badge marketing tone — Field Drawing idiom stays.
- No multi-video batch production — one good video before the next.
